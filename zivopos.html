Can I keep making sales when the internet goes down? Apakah transaksi kasir tetap bisa jalan saat internet mati? ▼

Yes. ZIVO can keep processing transactions while your internet connection is unavailable, so cashiers can continue serving customers without pause. Transactions are saved on the device and synced to your central reports automatically after the connection returns. Ya. ZIVO 100% offline-ready. Kasir tetap bisa input pesanan, buka laci kasir, dan cetak struk belanja walau tanpa internet. Saat koneksi online kembali, seluruh data transaksi otomatis terunggah ke laporan pusat tanpa perlu input ulang.

Which devices and printers can I use with ZIVO? Perangkat dan printer apa saja yang kompatibel dengan ZIVO? ▼

ZIVO works across PC computers, laptops, Android tablets, iPads, or smartphones via standard web browsers (Google Chrome or Safari). It connects with standard thermal receipt printers (Bluetooth, USB, or LAN) and barcode scanners. ZIVO fleksibel dan dapat digunakan di komputer PC, laptop, tablet Android, iPad, maupun smartphone lewat web browser standar. Kompatibel dengan printer struk thermal (Bluetooth, USB, LAN) dan scanner barcode standar.

Yes. You can separate Owner access (full access to financial reports, settings, and margins) and Cashier access (limited strictly to transaction checkout and receipt printing). Dilengkapi rekap uang kas awal & akhir shift untuk mencegah selisih laci.

Can I export sales and expense reports to Excel or PDF? Bisakah ekspor laporan penjualan & pengeluaran ke Excel atau PDF? ▼
