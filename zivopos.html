❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

PERTANYAAN UMUM SEPUTAR ZIVO POS

Can I keep making sales when the internet goes down? ▼

Yes. ZIVO can keep processing transactions while your internet connection is unavailable, so cashiers can continue serving customers without pause. Transactions are saved on the device and synced to your central reports automatically after the connection returns.

Which devices and printers can I use with ZIVO? ▼

ZIVO works across PC computers, laptops, Android tablets, iPads, or smartphones via standard web browsers (Google Chrome or Safari). It connects with standard thermal receipt printers (Bluetooth, USB, or LAN) and barcode scanners.

Can I have separate accounts for cashiers and managers? ▼

Yes. You can separate Owner access (full access to financial reports, settings, and margins) and Cashier access (limited strictly to transaction checkout and receipt printing). Shift handover tracking records cash in drawer at shift start and end.

Can I export sales and expense reports to Excel or PDF? ▼

Yes. From the Reports menu, you can filter sales and expense records by date, outlet, or category, and download them in Excel (XLSX) or print-ready PDF formats with one click.

How much is the subscription fee and installation? ▼

ZIVO provides transparent plans: Bronze (Rp 750.000 / 90 days), Gold (Rp 1.700.000 / 120 days with 30-day bonus), and Platinum (Rp 2.600.000 / 150 days with 60-day bonus). Zero transaction percentage deductions and free initial setup support.
